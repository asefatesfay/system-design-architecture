## 🎯 How to Use This Guide

1. **Sequential Learning**: Start from Section 1 and progress through each topic
2. **Hands-On Practice**: Write code for every example - don't just read
3. **Language Choice**: Pick Java, C++, Python, or C# and stick with it
4. **Deliberate Practice**: Solve problems without looking at solutions first
5. **Review and Iterate**: Revisit concepts and refactor your solutions

## 📖 Recommended Study Plan

### Week 1-2: Foundations
- Complete OOP Fundamentals
- Study Design Principles
- Practice with simple class designs

### Week 3-4: Patterns and UML
- Learn UML diagrams
- Master the 10 essential design patterns
- Apply patterns to small problems

### Week 5-6: Practice Problems
- Solve 2-3 problems per week
- Review multiple approaches
- Time yourself (45-60 min for OOD, 90-120 min for machine coding)

### Week 7-8: Mock Interviews
- Practice explaining your designs out loud
- Focus on trade-offs and alternatives
- Get feedback from peers

## 🎓 Success Tips

1. **Think Before Coding**: Always clarify requirements and design first
2. **Start Simple**: Begin with core functionality, then extend
3. **Explain Your Reasoning**: Interviewers care more about your thought process than perfect code
4. **Consider Trade-offs**: Every design decision has pros and cons
5. **Use SOLID**: Reference SOLID principles when explaining your design
6. **Don't Force Patterns**: Use patterns only when they genuinely improve the design
